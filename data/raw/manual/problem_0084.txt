ID: 0084

SOURCE: IMO Shortlist

YEAR: 2012

TOPIC: Number Theory

SUBTOPICS: Diophantine Equations, Parametric Constructions, Perfect Squares, Inequalities

STATEMENT:
An integer $a$ is called friendly if the equation
\[
(m^2+n)(n^2+m)=a(m-n)^3
\]
has a solution in positive integers $m,n$.

(a) Prove that there are at least $500$ friendly integers in the set $\{1,2,\ldots,2012\}$.

(b) Decide whether $a=2$ is friendly.

SOLUTION 1:
For part (a), every integer of the form
\[
a=4k-3,
\qquad k\ge2,
\]
is friendly. Indeed, take
\[
m=2k-1,
\qquad
n=k-1.
\]
Then $m-n=k$, and
\[
\begin{aligned}
(m^2+n)(n^2+m)
&=\bigl((2k-1)^2+k-1\bigr)\bigl((k-1)^2+2k-1\bigr)\\
&=k(4k-3)\cdot k^2\\
&=(4k-3)(m-n)^3.
\end{aligned}
\]
For $k=2,3,\ldots,503$, this gives the $502$ distinct friendly integers
\[
5,9,13,\ldots,2009.
\]
Thus there are at least $500$ friendly integers in the required set.

For part (b), we prove that $2$ is not friendly. Suppose, for a contradiction, that positive integers $m,n$ satisfy
\[
(m^2+n)(n^2+m)=2(m-n)^3.
\tag{1}
\]
The left-hand side is positive, so $m>n$.

Using the difference-of-squares identity, equation (1) gives
\[
\frac14\left((m^2+n+n^2+m)^2-(m^2+n-n^2-m)^2\right)=2(m-n)^3.
\]
Since
\[
m^2+n-n^2-m=(m-n)(m+n-1),
\]
we obtain
\[
(m^2+n+n^2+m)^2
=(m-n)^2\left((m+n-1)^2+8(m-n)\right).
\tag{2}
\]
It follows that
\[
Q=(m+n-1)^2+8(m-n)
\]
is a perfect square. Indeed, (2) says that $Q$ is the square of a rational number; an integer that is a rational square is an integer square.

Since $Q>(m+n-1)^2$ and the two squares have the same parity, there is an integer $s\ge1$ such that
\[
Q=(m+n-1+2s)^2.
\]
Subtracting $(m+n-1)^2$ and dividing by $4$ gives
\[
s(m+n-1+s)=2(m-n).
\tag{3}
\]
But
\[
m+n-1+s>m-n.
\]
If $s\ge2$, the left-hand side of (3) would be greater than $2(m-n)$. Hence $s=1$. Equation (3) then becomes
\[
m+n=2(m-n),
\]
so
\[
m=3n.
\]
Substituting this into (1), its right-hand side is
\[
2(2n)^3=16n^3.
\]
On the other hand,
\[
(m^2+n)(n^2+m)>m^2\cdot m=m^3=27n^3,
\]
a contradiction.

Therefore
\[
\boxed{a=2\text{ is not friendly}}.
\]
