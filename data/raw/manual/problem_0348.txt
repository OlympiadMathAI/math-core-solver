ID: 0348

SOURCE: IMO Shortlist

YEAR: 2021

TOPIC: Geometry

SUBTOPICS: Tangential Quadrilaterals, Arc Midpoints, Reflections, Similar Triangles

STATEMENT:
Let $ABCD$ be a convex quadrilateral circumscribed about a circle with centre $I$. Let $\omega$ be the circumcircle of triangle $ACI$.

The extensions of $BA$ and $BC$ beyond $A$ and $C$ meet $\omega$ at $X$ and $Z$, respectively. The extensions of $AD$ and $CD$ beyond $D$ meet $\omega$ at $Y$ and $T$, respectively.

Prove that the perimeters of the possibly self-intersecting quadrilaterals
\[
ADTX
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
CDYZ
\]
are equal.

SOLUTION 1:
Because $CI$ bisects the angle formed by $CB$ and $CD$, it is the external angle bisector of $\angle TCZ$. Since
\[
C,I,T,Z
\]
lie on $\omega$, the point $I$ is the midpoint of the corresponding arc $TZ$. Hence
\[
IT=IZ.
\tag{1}
\]
Similarly,
\[
IX=IY.
\tag{2}
\]

Let $O$ be the centre of $\omega$. Since both $O$ and $I$ are equidistant from $X$ and $Y$, the line $OI$ is the perpendicular bisector of $XY$. Likewise, it is the perpendicular bisector of $TZ$. Reflection in $OI$ therefore sends
\[
X\longleftrightarrow Y
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
T\longleftrightarrow Z.
\]
Consequently,
\[
XT=YZ.
\tag{3}
\]

Let the incircle of $ABCD$ touch
\[
AB,\ BC,\ CD,\ DA
\]
at
\[
P,\ Q,\ R,\ S,
\]
respectively.

The triangles $IXP$ and $IYS$ are right triangles. By (2) and
\[
IP=IS,
\]
they are congruent, so
\[
XP=YS.
\tag{4}
\]
Similarly, the right triangles $IRT$ and $IQZ$ are congruent by (1), and hence
\[
RT=QZ.
\tag{5}
\]

Using equal tangent lengths from the same vertex,
\[
AP=AS,\qquad CQ=CR,\qquad DS=DR.
\tag{6}
\]
Therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Per}(ADTX)
&=XT+XA+AD+DT\\
&=XT+XA+AS+SD+DT\\
&=XT+XP+RT\\
&=YZ+YS+QZ\\
&=YZ+YD+DR+RC+CZ\\
&=\operatorname{Per}(CDYZ),
\end{aligned}
\]
where we used (3)--(6). This proves the assertion.
