ID: 0077

SOURCE: IMO Shortlist

YEAR: 2012

TOPIC: Geometry

SUBTOPICS: Right Triangles, Reflections, Power of a Point, Tangent Circles, Equal Tangents

STATEMENT:
Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $\angle BCA=90^\circ$, and let $C_0$ be the foot of the altitude from $C$ to $AB$. Choose a point $X$ in the interior of the segment $CC_0$. Let $K$ and $L$ be the points on the segments $AX$ and $BX$, respectively, such that
\[
BK=BC
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
AL=AC.
\]
Let $M=AL\cap BK$. Prove that $MK=ML$.

SOLUTION 1:
Let $C'$ be the reflection of $C$ in the line $AB$. Since $CC_0\perp AB$, the points
\[
C,X,C_0,C'
\]
are collinear.

Let $\omega_1$ be the circle centered at $A$ through $L$, and let $\omega_2$ be the circle centered at $B$ through $K$. Since
\[
AL=AC=AC'
\]
and
\[
BK=BC=BC',
\]
we have
\[
C,C',L\in\omega_1,
\qquad
C,C',K\in\omega_2.
\]
Because $AC\perp BC$, the line $AC$ is tangent to $\omega_2$ at $C$, and the line $BC$ is tangent to $\omega_1$ at $C$.

Let $K_1\ne K$ be the second intersection of the line $AX$ with $\omega_2$, and let $L_1\ne L$ be the second intersection of the line $BX$ with $\omega_1$.

By the power of $X$ with respect to $\omega_2$,
\[
XK\cdot XK_1=XC\cdot XC'.
\tag{1}
\]
By the power of $X$ with respect to $\omega_1$,
\[
XL\cdot XL_1=XC\cdot XC'.
\tag{2}
\]
Hence
\[
XK\cdot XK_1=XL\cdot XL_1.
\]
Therefore the four points
\[
K,K_1,L,L_1
\]
are concyclic; denote their circle by $\omega_3$.

Now use the power of $A$ with respect to $\omega_2$. Since $AC$ is tangent to $\omega_2$ and the line $AKK_1$ is a secant,
\[
AK\cdot AK_1=AC^2=AL^2.
\tag{3}
\]
But $AK\cdot AK_1$ is also the power of $A$ with respect to $\omega_3$. Equation (3) therefore shows that the line $AL$ is tangent to $\omega_3$ at $L$.

Similarly, the line $BLL_1$ is a secant of $\omega_1$, while $BC$ is tangent to $\omega_1$ at $C$. Hence
\[
BL\cdot BL_1=BC^2=BK^2.
\tag{4}
\]
The left-hand side of (4) is the power of $B$ with respect to $\omega_3$. Therefore the line $BK$ is tangent to $\omega_3$ at $K$.

Since $M=AL\cap BK$, the segments $ML$ and $MK$ are tangent segments from the same point $M$ to the circle $\omega_3$. Therefore
\[
MK=ML.
\]
