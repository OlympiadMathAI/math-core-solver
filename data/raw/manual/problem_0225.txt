ID: 0225

SOURCE: IMO Shortlist

YEAR: 2017

TOPIC: Geometry

SUBTOPICS: Orthocenter, Circumcenter, Similar Triangles, Tangent-Chord Theorem, Median

STATEMENT:
Let $O$ be the circumcenter of an acute scalene triangle $ABC$. The line $OA$ meets the altitudes through $B$ and $C$ at $P$ and $Q$, respectively. These altitudes meet at the orthocenter $H$. Prove that the circumcenter of $\triangle PQH$ lies on a median of $\triangle ABC$.

SOLUTION 1:
Assume without loss of generality that
\[
AB<AC.
\]
Since $P,Q,A,O$ are collinear, while $QH\perp AB$ and $PH\perp AC$, we have
\[
\begin{aligned}
\angle PQH
&=90^\circ-\angle QAB\\
&=90^\circ-\angle OAB\\
&=\frac12\angle AOB\\
&=\angle ACB,
\end{aligned}
\]
and similarly
\[
\angle QPH=\angle ABC.
\]
Thus
\[
\triangle HPQ\sim\triangle ABC.
\tag{1}
\]

Let $\Omega$ and $\omega$ be the circumcircles of $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle HPQ$, respectively. Since
\[
\angle AHP
=90^\circ-\angle HAC
=\angle ACB
=\angle HQP,
\]
the line $AH$ is tangent to $\omega$ at $H$.

Let $T$ be the center of $\omega$, and let
\[
M=AT\cap BC.
\]
Under the similarity (1), the point $H$ corresponds to $A$, the line $PQ$ corresponds to $BC$, and the tangent $AH$ at $H$ corresponds to a tangent to $\Omega$ at $A$. Let that tangent meet $BC$ at $S$. The centers $T$ and $O$ correspond, while the points $A\in PQ$ and $S\in BC$ correspond. Therefore
\[
\angle OSM=\angle OAT.
\]
Since $A,T,M$ are collinear,
\[
\angle OAT=\angle OAM.
\]
Hence
\[
\angle OSM=\angle OAM,
\]
so $S,A,O,M$ are concyclic.

The line $AS$ is tangent to $\Omega$ at $A$, so
\[
AS\perp AO.
\]
Thus
\[
\angle OMS
=180^\circ-\angle OAS
=90^\circ.
\]
Since $M,S\in BC$, this means
\[
OM\perp BC.
\]
The perpendicular from the circumcenter $O$ to $BC$ passes through the midpoint of $BC$. Therefore $M$ is the midpoint of $BC$, and $T$ lies on the median $AM$. Since $T$ is the circumcenter of $\triangle PQH$, the result follows.
