ID: 0363

SOURCE: IMO Shortlist

YEAR: 2022

TOPIC: Algebra

SUBTOPICS: Sequences, Inequalities, Extremal Arguments, Contradiction

STATEMENT:
Let $(a_n)_{n\ge1}$ be a sequence of positive real numbers such that
\[
(a_{n+1})^2+a_na_{n+2}\le a_n+a_{n+2}
\tag{1}
\]
for every positive integer $n$. Prove that
\[
a_{2022}\le1.
\]

SOLUTION 1:
Rearranging (1), we obtain
\[
(a_{n+1})^2-1
\le a_n+a_{n+2}-a_na_{n+2}-1
=(1-a_n)(a_{n+2}-1).
\tag{2}
\]

We first show that two consecutive terms among
\[
a_2,a_3,a_4,\ldots
\]
cannot both be greater than $1$.

Suppose, to the contrary, that
\[
a_{n+1}>1
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
a_{n+2}>1
\]
for some positive integer $n$. The left-hand side of (2) is positive, so
\[
0<a_n<1.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\begin{aligned}
(a_{n+1})^2-1
&\le(1-a_n)(a_{n+2}-1)\\
&<(1+a_{n+2})(a_{n+2}-1)\\
&=(a_{n+2})^2-1.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
\]

Applying (2) with $n+1$ in place of $n$, we have
\[
(a_{n+2})^2-1
\le(1-a_{n+1})(a_{n+3}-1).
\]
Since the left-hand side is positive and $1-a_{n+1}<0$, we must have
\[
a_{n+3}<1.
\]
Therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
(a_{n+2})^2-1
&\le(a_{n+1}-1)(1-a_{n+3})\\
&<(a_{n+1}-1)(a_{n+1}+1)\\
&=(a_{n+1})^2-1,
\end{aligned}
\]
contradicting (3). This proves the claim.

Now suppose that $a_{2022}>1$. The claim gives
\[
a_{2021}\le1
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
a_{2023}\le1.
\]
Using (2) with $n=2021$, we obtain
\[
0<(a_{2022})^2-1
\le(1-a_{2021})(a_{2023}-1)
\le0,
\]
a contradiction. Hence
\[
\boxed{a_{2022}\le1}.
\]
