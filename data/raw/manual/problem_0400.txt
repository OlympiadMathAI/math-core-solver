ID: 0400

SOURCE: IMO Shortlist

YEAR: 2023

TOPIC: Algebra

SUBTOPICS: Permutations, Triangle Inequality, Extremal Sums, Graceful Permutations

STATEMENT:
Let
\[
a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_{2023}
\]
be positive integers such that

\begin{itemize}
\item $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_{2023}$ is a permutation of $1,2,\ldots,2023$;
\item
\[
|a_1-a_2|,|a_2-a_3|,\ldots,|a_{2022}-a_{2023}|
\]
is a permutation of $1,2,\ldots,2022$.
\end{itemize}

Prove that
\[
\max(a_1,a_{2023})\ge507.
\]

SOLUTION 1:
We prove the following more general statement.

Let $N$ be a positive integer, and let
\[
a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_{2N-1}
\]
be a permutation of $1,2,\ldots,2N-1$ whose consecutive absolute differences are a permutation of
\[
1,2,\ldots,2N-2.
\]
Then
\[
a_1+a_{2N-1}\ge N+1.
\tag{1}
\]

For
\[
a\in\{1,2,\ldots,2N-1\},
\]
define its score by
\[
s(a)=|a-N|.
\]
The triangle inequality gives
\[
|a-b|\le s(a)+s(b).
\]
Therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
(N-1)(2N-1)
&=
\sum_{i=1}^{2N-2}|a_i-a_{i+1}|\\
&\le
2\sum_{i=1}^{2N-1}s(a_i)
-s(a_1)-s(a_{2N-1}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
The scores form the multiset
\[
0,1,1,2,2,\ldots,N-1,N-1,
\]
so
\[
\sum_{i=1}^{2N-1}s(a_i)=N(N-1).
\]
Substituting this into (2),
\[
s(a_1)+s(a_{2N-1})\le N-1.
\tag{3}
\]
Since
\[
N-a\le|a-N|=s(a)
\]
for every $a$, equation (3) gives
\[
(N-a_1)+(N-a_{2N-1})\le N-1.
\]
This is equivalent to (1).

For the problem, take
\[
2N-1=2023,
\qquad N=1012.
\]
Then
\[
a_1+a_{2023}\ge1013,
\]
and hence
\[
\max(a_1,a_{2023})
\ge
\left\lceil\frac{1013}{2}\right\rceil
=
507.
\]
