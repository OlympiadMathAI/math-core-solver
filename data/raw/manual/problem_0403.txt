ID: 0403

SOURCE: IMO Shortlist

YEAR: 2023

TOPIC: Combinatorics

SUBTOPICS: Grid Colourings, Parity Invariants, Constructive Moves, Modular Arithmetic

STATEMENT:
Let $m,n>1$ be positive integers. Every unit square of an $m\times n$ grid contains a coin, initially showing tails.

A move consists of the following steps:

\begin{enumerate}
\item select a $2\times2$ subgrid;
\item flip the coins in its top-left and bottom-right squares;
\item flip the coin in exactly one of its top-right and bottom-left squares.
\end{enumerate}

Determine all pairs $(m,n)$ for which it is possible, after finitely many moves, to make every coin show heads.

SOLUTION 1:
The answer is
\[
\boxed{3\mid mn}.
\]

We first prove sufficiency. By symmetry, assume
\[
3\mid m.
\]
Number the rows and columns from top to bottom and from left to right.

For
\[
1\le i<m,\qquad 1\le j<n,
\]
let $A(i,j)$ be the move that flips
\[
(i,j),\qquad(i,j+1),\qquad(i+1,j+1),
\]
and let $B(i,j)$ be the move that flips
\[
(i,j),\qquad(i+1,j),\qquad(i+1,j+1).
\]

Suppose first that $n$ is even. For every
\[
1\le r\le\frac m3,
\qquad
1\le s\le\frac n2,
\]
perform
\[
A(3r-2,2s-1)
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
B(3r-1,2s-1).
\]
Inside each corresponding $3\times2$ block, these two moves flip every coin exactly once. Hence all coins end showing heads.

Now suppose that $n$ is odd. Apply the preceding construction to the first $n-1$ columns. At that point precisely the coins in the last column still show tails. For each
\[
1\le r\le\frac m3,
\]
perform
\[
A(3r-2,n-1),\qquad
A(3r-1,n-1),\qquad
B(3r-2,n-1).
\]
Within the $3\times2$ block formed by rows $3r-2,3r-1,3r$ and columns $n-1,n$, every coin in column $n-1$ is flipped an even number of times, while every coin in column $n$ is flipped an odd number of times. Thus all coins now show heads.

We next prove necessity. Label square $(i,j)$ by
\[
i+j-2\pmod3.
\]
For $r\in\{0,1,2\}$, let $T_r$ be the number of head-up coins in squares labelled $r$.

Every legal move flips exactly one square of each label. Consequently, the parities of
\[
T_0-T_1
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
T_1-T_2
\]
are invariant. Initially
\[
T_0=T_1=T_2=0,
\]
so every reachable configuration must satisfy
\[
T_0\equiv T_1\equiv T_2\pmod2.
\tag{1}
\]

In the all-head configuration, $T_r$ equals the number of squares with label $r$. If neither $m$ nor $n$ is divisible by $3$, these three numbers are as follows:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
(m\bmod3,n\bmod3)&(T_0,T_1,T_2)\\ \hline
(1,1)&
\left(\dfrac{mn+2}{3},\dfrac{mn-1}{3},\dfrac{mn-1}{3}\right)\\[2mm]
(1,2)\text{ or }(2,1)&
\left(\dfrac{mn+1}{3},\dfrac{mn+1}{3},\dfrac{mn-2}{3}\right)\\[2mm]
(2,2)&
\left(\dfrac{mn-1}{3},\dfrac{mn+2}{3},\dfrac{mn-1}{3}\right).
\end{array}
\]
In every case, two displayed values differ by $1$, so they cannot all have the same parity. This contradicts (1).

Therefore at least one of $m,n$ is divisible by $3$, which is equivalent to
\[
3\mid mn.
\]
