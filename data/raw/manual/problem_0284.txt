ID: 0284

SOURCE: IMO Shortlist

YEAR: 2019

TOPIC: Geometry

SUBTOPICS: Orthic Triangle, Incircles, Similar Right Triangles, Chord Lengths, Sine Rule

STATEMENT:
Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle, and let $D,E,F$ be the feet of the altitudes from $A,B,C$ to $BC,CA,AB$, respectively. Let $\omega_B$ and $\omega_C$ be the incircles of triangles $BDF$ and $CDE$. Suppose that $\omega_B$ is tangent to $DF$ at $M$, and $\omega_C$ is tangent to $DE$ at $N$. The line $MN$ meets $\omega_B$ again at $P\ne M$ and $\omega_C$ again at $Q\ne N$. Prove that
\[
MP=NQ.
\]

SOLUTION 1:
Let $O_B,O_C$ and $r_B,r_C$ be the centres and radii of $\omega_B,\omega_C$, respectively. Let the two circles touch $BC$ at $T$ and $U$.

The quadrilaterals $AFDC$ and $ABDE$ are cyclic. Therefore
\[
\angle MDO_B
=\frac12\angle FDB
=\frac12\angle BAC
=\frac12\angle CDE
=\angle O_CDN.
\]
The triangles $DMO_B$ and $DNO_C$ are right triangles, so they are similar. Consequently,
\[
\frac{DN}{DM}
=\frac{O_CN}{O_BM}
=\frac{r_C}{r_B}.
\tag{1}
\]

Set
\[
\varphi=\angle DMN,
\qquad
\psi=\angle MND.
\]
Because $FM$ and $EN$ are tangents to $\omega_B$ and $\omega_C$, the tangent-chord theorem gives
\[
\angle MTP=\angle FMP=\varphi,
\qquad
\angle QUN=\angle QNE=\psi,
\]
with directed angles if one of the points lies in a limiting position.

A chord of a circle of radius $r$ which subtends an inscribed angle $\theta$ has length $2r\sin\theta$. Hence
\[
MP=2r_B\sin\varphi,
\qquad
NQ=2r_C\sin\psi.
\tag{2}
\]
By the sine rule in triangle $DMN$,
\[
\frac{DN}{DM}=\frac{\sin\varphi}{\sin\psi}.
\tag{3}
\]
Combining (1)--(3),
\[
\frac{MP}{NQ}
=\frac{r_B}{r_C}\cdot\frac{\sin\varphi}{\sin\psi}
=\frac{DM}{DN}\cdot\frac{DN}{DM}
=1.
\]
Therefore $MP=NQ$.
