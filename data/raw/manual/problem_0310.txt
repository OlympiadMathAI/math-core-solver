ID: 0310

SOURCE: IMO Shortlist

YEAR: 2020

TOPIC: Combinatorics

SUBTOPICS: Fibonacci Numbers, Graphs, Acyclic Graphs, Extremal Set Construction

STATEMENT:
The Fibonacci numbers are defined by
\[
F_0=0,\qquad F_1=1,\qquad F_{m+1}=F_m+F_{m-1}\quad(m\ge1).
\]
Given an integer $n\ge2$, determine the smallest possible size of a set $S$ of integers such that, for every
\[
k=2,3,\ldots,n,
\]
there are $x,y\in S$ satisfying
\[
x-y=F_k.
\]

SOLUTION 1:
The answer is
\[
\boxed{\left\lceil\frac n2\right\rceil+1}.
\]

Put
\[
d=\left\lceil\frac n2\right\rceil,
\]
so
\[
n\le2d\le n+1.
\]

We first prove the lower bound. Suppose $S$ satisfies the required condition. Construct a graph whose vertices are the elements of $S$. For every
\[
k=1,2,\ldots,d,
\]
choose an edge whose endpoints differ by
\[
F_{2k-1}.
\]
This is possible: for $k\ge2$, we have
\[
3\le2k-1\le2d-1\le n,
\]
while for $k=1$ we use
\[
F_1=F_2=1.
\]
The $d$ chosen edge lengths
\[
F_1,F_3,\ldots,F_{2d-1}
\]
are distinct.

We claim that this graph is acyclic. Suppose it contained a cycle, and let its longest edge have length
\[
F_{2m+1}.
\]
All other edges of the cycle have distinct lengths chosen from
\[
F_1,F_3,\ldots,F_{2m-1}.
\]
By the triangle inequality,
\[
F_{2m+1}
\le
F_1+F_3+\cdots+F_{2m-1}.
\]
But
\[
\begin{aligned}
F_1+F_3+\cdots+F_{2m-1}
&=F_2+(F_4-F_2)+\cdots+(F_{2m}-F_{2m-2})\\
&=F_{2m}<F_{2m+1},
\end{aligned}
\]
a contradiction.

An acyclic graph with $d$ edges has at least $d+1$ vertices. Therefore
\[
|S|\ge d+1.
\]

For the matching construction, take
\[
S=\{F_0,F_2,F_4,\ldots,F_{2d}\}.
\]
This set has $d+1$ elements. For every $1\le k\le d$,
\[
F_{2k}-F_{2k-2}=F_{2k-1}
\]
and
\[
F_{2k}-F_0=F_{2k}.
\]
Thus every Fibonacci number
\[
F_1,F_2,\ldots,F_{2d}
\]
occurs as a difference of two elements of $S$. Since $n\le2d$, all the required differences occur.

Hence the minimum size is
\[
\boxed{\left\lceil\frac n2\right\rceil+1}.
\]
