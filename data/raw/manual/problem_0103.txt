ID: 0103

SOURCE: IMO Shortlist

YEAR: 2013

TOPIC: Geometry

SUBTOPICS: Orthocenter, Radical Axes, Power of a Point, Cyclic Quadrilaterals, Similar Triangles

STATEMENT:
Let $ABC$ be an acute-angled triangle with orthocenter $H$, and let $W$ be a point on side $BC$. Let $M$ and $N$ be the feet of the altitudes from $B$ and $C$, respectively. Let $\omega_1$ be the circumcircle of $BWN$, and let $X$ be the point of $\omega_1$ diametrically opposite $W$. Analogously, let $\omega_2$ be the circumcircle of $CWM$, and let $Y$ be the point of $\omega_2$ diametrically opposite $W$. Prove that $X,Y,H$ are collinear.

SOLUTION 1:
Let $L$ be the foot of the altitude from $A$, and let $Z\ne W$ be the second common point of $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$. We will prove that $X,Y,Z,H$ lie on one line.

Since
\[
\angle BNC=\angle BMC=90^\circ,
\]
the points $B,C,N,M$ are concyclic; denote their circle by $\omega_3$. The radical axes of the three circles $\omega_1,\omega_2,\omega_3$ are
\[
WZ,\qquad BN,\qquad CM,
\]
respectively. Because
\[
A=BN\cap CM,
\]
the radical-center theorem gives
\[
A\in WZ.
\tag{1}
\]

The segments $WX$ and $WY$ are diameters of $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$. Hence
\[
\angle WZX=\angle WZY=90^\circ.
\]
Thus $X,Y,Z$ lie on the line through $Z$ perpendicular to $WZ$.

It remains to place $H$ on the same line. The quadrilateral $BLHN$ is cyclic, since
\[
\angle BLH=\angle BNH=90^\circ.
\]
Taking powers of $A$ with respect to $BLHN$ and $\omega_1$, we obtain
\[
AL\cdot AH=AB\cdot AN=AW\cdot AZ.
\tag{2}
\]

If $H$ lies on $AW$, then (1) shows that $A,W,H,Z$ are collinear, and (2) gives $H=Z$. Hence the conclusion follows.

Suppose now that $H$ does not lie on $AW$. From (2),
\[
\frac{AZ}{AH}=\frac{AL}{AW}.
\]
Moreover,
\[
\angle HAZ=\angle LAW,
\]
because $AH\perp BC$, $AL\perp BC$, and $A,Z,W$ are collinear. Therefore
\[
\triangle AHZ\sim\triangle AWL.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\angle HZA=\angle WLA=90^\circ.
\]
By (1), this means that $HZ\perp WZ$. Hence $H$ lies on the same line through $Z$ as $X$ and $Y$, and therefore $X,Y,H$ are collinear.
