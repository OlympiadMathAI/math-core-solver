ID: 0268

SOURCE: IMO Shortlist

YEAR: 2019

TOPIC: Algebra

SUBTOPICS: Inequalities, Convexity, Extremal Values, Cauchy-Schwarz

STATEMENT:
Let $u_1,u_2,\ldots,u_{2019}$ be real numbers satisfying
\[
u_1+u_2+\cdots+u_{2019}=0
\]
and
\[
u_1^2+u_2^2+\cdots+u_{2019}^2=1.
\]
Let
\[
a=\min(u_1,u_2,\ldots,u_{2019}),
\qquad
b=\max(u_1,u_2,\ldots,u_{2019}).
\]
Prove that
\[
ab\le-\frac1{2019}.
\]

SOLUTION 1:
The numbers $u_i$ are not all zero, and their sum is zero. Hence
\[
a<0<b.
\]
Let
\[
P=\{i:u_i>0\},
\qquad
N=\{i:u_i\le0\},
\]
and write
\[
p=|P|,
\qquad
q=|N|.
\]
Then $p+q=2019$. Since the total sum is zero,
\[
\sum_{i\in P}u_i=\sum_{i\in N}|u_i|.
\tag{1}
\]

For $i\in P$, we have $0<u_i\le b$, and therefore
\[
u_i^2\le bu_i.
\]
Using (1),
\[
\sum_{i\in P}u_i^2
\le b\sum_{i\in P}u_i
=b\sum_{i\in N}|u_i|
\le bq|a|
=-qab.
\tag{2}
\]
Similarly, for $i\in N$ we have $|u_i|\le|a|$, so
\[
\sum_{i\in N}u_i^2
\le |a|\sum_{i\in N}|u_i|
=|a|\sum_{i\in P}u_i
\le |a|pb
=-pab.
\tag{3}
\]
Adding (2) and (3),
\[
1=\sum_{i=1}^{2019}u_i^2
\le-(p+q)ab
=-2019ab.
\]
Thus
\[
ab\le-\frac1{2019}.
\]

SOLUTION 2:
Again, $a<0<b$. Every $u_i$ is a convex combination of $a$ and $b$, so there are numbers $x_i,y_i\in[0,1]$ with
\[
x_i+y_i=1
\]
and
\[
u_i=x_i a+y_i b.
\]
Put
\[
X=\sum_{i=1}^{2019}x_i,
\qquad
Y=\sum_{i=1}^{2019}y_i.
\]
The conditions $\sum u_i=0$ and $\sum(x_i+y_i)=2019$ give
\[
|a|X=bY,
\qquad
X+Y=2019.
\]
Solving,
\[
X=\frac{2019b}{|a|+b},
\qquad
Y=\frac{2019|a|}{|a|+b}.
\tag{1}
\]

Since the function $t\mapsto t^2$ is convex,
\[
u_i^2=(x_i a+y_i b)^2\le x_i a^2+y_i b^2.
\]
Therefore, by (1),
\[
\begin{aligned}
1
&=\sum_{i=1}^{2019}u_i^2\\
&\le Xa^2+Yb^2\\
&=\frac{2019b}{|a|+b}|a|^2
 +\frac{2019|a|}{|a|+b}b^2\\
&=2019|a|b
=-2019ab.
\end{aligned}
\]
Hence
\[
ab\le-\frac1{2019}.
\]
