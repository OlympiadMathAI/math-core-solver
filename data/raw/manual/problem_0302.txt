ID: 0302

SOURCE: IMO Shortlist

YEAR: 2020

TOPIC: Algebra

SUBTOPICS: Weighted AM-GM, Ordered Variables, Convexity, Logarithmic Inequalities

STATEMENT:
Let $a,b,c,d$ be real numbers satisfying
\[
a\ge b\ge c\ge d>0
\]
and
\[
a+b+c+d=1.
\]
Prove that
\[
(a+2b+3c+4d)a^ab^bc^cd^d<1.
\]

SOLUTION 1:
The weighted arithmetic-geometric mean inequality, with weights $a,b,c,d$, gives
\[
a^ab^bc^cd^d
\le a\cdot a+b\cdot b+c\cdot c+d\cdot d
=a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2.
\tag{1}
\]
We prove
\[
(a+2b+3c+4d)(a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2)<1.
\tag{2}
\]
Since all four variables are positive,
\[
\begin{aligned}
(a+b+c+d)^3
&>
 a^2(a+3b+3c+3d)\\
&\quad+b^2(3a+b+3c+3d)\\
&\quad+c^2(3a+3b+c+3d)\\
&\quad+d^2(3a+3b+3c+d).
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
\]
The ordering $a\ge b\ge c\ge d$ implies that each coefficient in the large expression on the right of (3) is at least $a+2b+3c+4d$. Hence
\[
(a+b+c+d)^3
>
(a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2)(a+2b+3c+4d).
\]
Because $a+b+c+d=1$, this is (2). Combining (1) and (2) gives the desired strict inequality.

SOLUTION 2:
Since $b\ge d$,
\[
a+2b+3c+4d
\le a+3b+3c+3d
=3-2a.
\tag{1}
\]

Suppose first that $a<1/2$. Because $a$ is the largest of the four variables,
\[
a^ab^bc^cd^d
\le a^{a+b+c+d}=a.
\]
Thus, by (1),
\[
(a+2b+3c+4d)a^ab^bc^cd^d
\le(3-2a)a
=1-(1-a)(1-2a)<1.
\]

Now assume
\[
\frac12\le a<1.
\]
Each of $b,c,d$ is strictly smaller than $1-a=b+c+d$, so
\[
b^bc^cd^d<(1-a)^{b+c+d}=(1-a)^{1-a}.
\]
Consequently,
\[
(a+2b+3c+4d)a^ab^bc^cd^d
<
(3-2a)a^a(1-a)^{1-a}.
\tag{2}
\]
Define, for $0<x<1$,
\[
F(x)=(3-2x)x^x(1-x)^{1-x}
\]
and
\[
G(x)=\log F(x)
=\log(3-2x)+x\log x+(1-x)\log(1-x).
\]
A calculation gives
\[
G''(x)
=
\frac1{x(1-x)}-\frac4{(3-2x)^2}
=
\frac{1+8(1-x)^2}{x(1-x)(3-2x)^2}>0.
\]
Thus $G$ is strictly convex. Also,
\[
G\left(\frac12\right)=0
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\lim_{x\to1^-}G(x)=0.
\]
A convex function lies below the chord joining two endpoint values, so
\[
G(x)\le0
\qquad\left(\frac12\le x<1\right).
\]
Hence $F(x)\le1$ on this interval. Applying this to (2) gives the required strict inequality.
