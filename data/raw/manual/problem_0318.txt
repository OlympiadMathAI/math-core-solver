ID: 0318

SOURCE: IMO Shortlist

YEAR: 2020

TOPIC: Geometry

SUBTOPICS: Disjoint Disks, Induction, Triangle Inequality, Extremal Geometry

STATEMENT:
In the plane, let
\[
D_1,D_2,\ldots,D_n
\qquad(n\ge6)
\]
be pairwise disjoint disks with radii
\[
R_1\ge R_2\ge\cdots\ge R_n.
\]
For every $i$, choose a point $P_i\in D_i$, and let $O$ be an arbitrary point in the plane.

Prove that
\[
OP_1+OP_2+\cdots+OP_n
\ge
R_6+R_7+\cdots+R_n.
\]

SOLUTION 1:
We use the following lemma.

\textbf{Lemma.}
Let $D_1,\ldots,D_6$ be six pairwise disjoint disks with radii $R_1,\ldots,R_6$. Choose $P_i\in D_i$, and let $O$ be any point. Then there are indices $i,j$ such that
\[
OP_i\ge R_j.
\]

\textit{Proof.}
Let $O_i$ be the centre of $D_i$. Consider the six rays
\[
OO_1,OO_2,\ldots,OO_6.
\]
If $O=O_i$, assign an arbitrary direction to the corresponding ray. These rays determine six successive angles whose total measure is $360^\circ$, so one of them, say
\[
\angle O_iOO_j,
\]
is at most $60^\circ$.

In the possibly degenerate triangle $OO_iO_j$, the side $O_iO_j$ cannot be the unique longest side, because the angle opposite it is at most $60^\circ$. Interchanging $i$ and $j$ if necessary,
\[
OO_i\ge O_iO_j.
\]
The disks are disjoint, so
\[
O_iO_j\ge R_i+R_j.
\]
Since $P_i\in D_i$,
\[
OP_i
\ge OO_i-O_iP_i
\ge OO_i-R_i
\ge R_j.
\]
This proves the lemma. $\square$

We now prove the result by induction on $n\ge5$. For $n=5$, the right-hand side is an empty sum, so the assertion is trivial.

Let $n\ge6$, and assume the result for $n-1$ disks. Apply the lemma to the six disks with largest radii. It gives indices
\[
1\le i,j\le6
\]
such that
\[
OP_i\ge R_j\ge R_6.
\tag{1}
\]

Remove the disk $D_i$. The induction hypothesis applied to the remaining $n-1$ disks gives
\[
\sum_{k\ne i}OP_k
\ge
R_7+R_8+\cdots+R_n.
\tag{2}
\]
Indeed, after one of the six largest disks has been removed, the tail of the remaining ordered radii beginning with the sixth one is
\[
R_7,R_8,\ldots,R_n;
\]
when $n=6$, this tail is empty.

Adding (1) and (2), we obtain
\[
\sum_{k=1}^n OP_k
\ge
R_6+R_7+\cdots+R_n,
\]
as required.
